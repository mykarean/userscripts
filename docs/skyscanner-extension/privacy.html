Privacy Policy – Cheapest Round-Trip Dates Finder

Browser extension · Last updated: 2026-11-20

Summary

This extension does not collect, store on any server, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no backend, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party services.

What the extension does

On Skyscanner flight search pages (skyscanner.*/transport/…) the extension scans date combinations for the route you opened and shows the cheapest round trip. To do so it reads the flight prices and layover information that Skyscanner itself returns to your browser for that route.

Data processed

  • Flight search data from Skyscanner (prices, dates, layovers): processed locally in your browser only, to display results. It is not sent anywhere else.
  • Settings (for example the selected language or search range): stored locally in your browser (localStorage of the Skyscanner page, keys starting with gm_). This data never leaves your device and can be removed by clearing the site data for skyscanner.* or by uninstalling the extension.

Data sharing

None. No data is transmitted to the developer or to any third party. The extension makes no network requests to servers other than the Skyscanner pages you are already visiting.

Permissions

The extension requests no browser API permissions. Its only access is to Skyscanner flight search pages (skyscanner.*/transport/*), which it needs to read the search results shown there.
